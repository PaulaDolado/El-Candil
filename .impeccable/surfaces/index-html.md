# Surface: home (index.html)

Scope: single-page site, visitor mode Persuade (with Operate inside the booking form and Read inside the menu).
Audience/job: diners on a phone checking dishes and prices, then booking a table via WhatsApp.
Constraints: user-pinned palette (Champagne #EACEAA, Honey Garlic #85431E, Whiskey Sour #D39858, Burnt Coffee #34150F, Balsamico #150C0C), Pencerio display face, menu as filing-cabinet folders with clickable tabs. Sample data only, in js/data.js.
Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is printed as a vermouth-bottle label and enamel bar sign: double-rule borders, typographic medals, huge script name. Refuses the photo-hero + list-menu + generic form arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Balsamico ground, Champagne label stock, Burnt Coffee ink, Honey Garlic and Whiskey Sour as second inks. Thick double-rule frames with corner notches, circular stamp medals, small-caps condensed labels, Pencerio script for names. Each section prints with two inks only. No shadows, gradients or rounded cards; hierarchy by scale.

STORY: Visitor sees El Candil as a label, opens the cabinet, pulls a folder tab, reads dishes with prices and allergens, fills a booking ticket that confirms itself live, sends it to WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Balsamico; centred Champagne label with double frame; line-drawn candil above "El Candil" in Pencerio at ~18vw; tagline in small caps; two stamp buttons: Reservar mesa (primary, Whiskey Sour) and Ver la carta.

FORM: Etiqueta de vermut / chapa esmaltada, position 7 of the ordered list, seed key 59935fd8. Signature interaction: folder tabs that lift and open their folder; booking ticket that fills live.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
