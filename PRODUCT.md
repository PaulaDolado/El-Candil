# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step and no dependencies (user choice). Deployable to any static host.

## Users

Diners (locals and visitors) who want to check the menu of El Candil and book a table, mostly from a phone, often shortly before deciding where to eat. Families with children are part of the audience (there is a children's menu).

## Product Purpose

Public website of El Candil, a restaurant. Two jobs: read the menu (carta) easily, and request a table reservation. Success = a visitor finds a dish and its price in a few taps and sends a reservation without friction.

## Positioning

Undecided. No confirmed positioning beyond the name "El Candil" and a traditional Spanish kitchen inferred from the menu sections.

## Operating Context

- No backend. Reservations are sent via WhatsApp: the form composes a pre-filled message to the restaurant's WhatsApp number (user choice).
- The menu is organised in sections: Bebidas, Menú del día, Entrantes, Platos de carne, Platos de pescado, Otros primeros, Infantil, Postres.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Menu displayed as a filing cabinet / folder metaphor: each section is a folder whose tab, when clicked, opens that section (user requirement). It must stay easy to understand.
- All restaurant data (address, phone, WhatsApp number, hours, dishes, prices) are SAMPLE values for now, kept in a single editable data file. Replace before launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: El Candil.
- Palette provided by the user (binding): Champagne #EACEAA, Honey Garlic #85431E, Whiskey Sour #D39858, Burnt Coffee #34150F, Balsamico #150C0C.
- Display typeface: Pencerio (Fontshare), user choice.
- References supplied: a warm amber palette over a dark wicker-lamp photo; a retro cocktail-bar menu card ("Olive") with bold type and a script accent; a poster ("La mezcla") with a line-drawn cocktail illustration and handwritten script over a sunny terrace bar.

## Evidence on Hand

No photos, logo, reviews, awards or press. Do not fabricate testimonials, ratings or awards.

## Product Principles

1. The menu is the product: legible, scannable, prices always visible.
2. Booking in under a minute on a phone.
3. Warmth of a family restaurant over luxury pretension.
4. Sample data must be obviously easy to replace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish-language site. Menu tabs must be keyboard and screen-reader operable (tablist semantics). Allergen info should be shown per dish.
